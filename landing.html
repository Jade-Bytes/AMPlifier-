Questions before you start.

Is AMPlifier free?

Yes. Downloading the app and signing up are both free.

Will my grade match my report card?

AMPlifier calculates from the scores and weights you enter, so treat its numbers as estimates. Your teacher's grade is the official one. When you get it, enter it and it replaces the estimate for that term.

What if my school uses different weights?

Set the written work, performance task and exam weights for each subject. You can also turn on the DepEd transmutation table in Settings.

Who can see my grades?

Your workspace is private to your Google account. Community profiles are optional, so nothing is shared there unless you opt in.

Can I keep a copy of my records?

Yes. Settings lets you download a full JSON backup, or your assessments and report card as CSV files for spreadsheets.

Why does my device warn me about the download?

The Android app is not on Google Play and the Windows installer is not code-signed yet, so both systems ask you to confirm before installing.
